      HOW TO PLAY

      The Basics

      Monsters carry words down the screen. Type a word to slay it. If one crosses the red line at the bottom, it hits you.

      Just start typing — the first letter locks onto a target automatically.

      Controls
      A-ZType the falling words
      EnterUltimate — clears every enemy (needs full mana)
      Ctrl+1/2/3Fire skill slots 1–3
      EscPause menu

      Skills and the ultimate are also clickable if a shortcut is taken by your browser.

      Combos & Levels

      Consecutive kills build a combo, multiplying score. A mistyped letter or a missed word breaks it.

      Kills grant XP. Each level-up lets you draft a skill — three active slots, then permanent passives.

      Adventure

      Endless. Every level shifts to a new biome, and a boss guards every 5th level. Bosses hurl long phrases — typing them damages the boss.

      Royal Rumble

      Up to 6 fighters. Combos launch words at a random rival; whatever they fail to clear drains their health. Last one standing wins.
